WHAT WE DO

Our Key Areas

PPR works through community-led initiatives focused on education, rights, leadership, peacebuilding, support, and empowerment.

01

Education

Supporting learning opportunities for Rohingya children and youth through educational activities, learning materials, and teacher support.

02

Human Rights Advocacy

Promoting awareness, dignity, peaceful advocacy, and understanding of the rights of Rohingya refugees.

03

Leadership Development

Encouraging Rohingya youth and women to develop leadership skills and participate positively in their communities.

04

Peacebuilding

Promoting unity, dialogue, peaceful participation, and non-violent engagement within the community.

05

Community Support

Supporting vulnerable community members and coordinating practical assistance during times of need.

06

Community Empowerment

Strengthening skills, self-reliance, participation, and opportunities for Rohingya refugees.
