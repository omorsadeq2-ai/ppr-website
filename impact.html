OUR IMPACT

Creating Positive Change Together

PPR works with Rohingya community members to create opportunities for learning, leadership, participation, support, and community empowerment.

COMMUNITY-LED IMPACT

From Community Needs to Community Action

PPR responds to community needs through practical, peaceful, and community-led activities. Our work focuses on strengthening education, developing skills, supporting vulnerable people, encouraging leadership, and building awareness.

Through these activities, PPR seeks to create opportunities for Rohingya refugees to participate, learn, support one another, and contribute to a stronger community.

WHERE WE MAKE AN IMPACT

Our Areas of Impact

Our programs and activities contribute to different areas of community life.

01

Education

PPR supports learning opportunities, educational activities, teacher support, learning materials, and community-based education.

02

Women's Empowerment

Skills development activities provide opportunities for Rohingya women and girls to learn practical skills and participate in community life.

03

Youth Development

Youth activities encourage leadership, teamwork, sports, skills development, and positive participation within the community.

04

Humanitarian Support

PPR supports vulnerable Rohingya community members facing emergencies, displacement, housing difficulties, and other humanitarian challenges.

05

Digital Literacy

Digital literacy activities help Rohingya youth develop practical computer skills and improve their ability to use digital tools.

06

Community Awareness

PPR promotes awareness about education, human rights, peace, community unity, Rohingya history, and peaceful community participation.

OUR APPROACH

How We Work

PPR's approach is centered on listening to community needs and working together on practical solutions.

01

Listen

We listen to community members and identify needs, challenges, and opportunities.

02

Support

We organize practical activities and support for people and families who need assistance.

03

Empower

We create opportunities for education, skills development, leadership, and community participation.

04

Connect

We encourage cooperation with humanitarian organizations, community partners, and other stakeholders.
