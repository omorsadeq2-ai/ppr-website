WHO WE ARE

A Refugee-Led Community Initiative

Peace & Progress for Rohingya (PPR) is a refugee-led community initiative established in 2023 by Rohingya refugees in Indonesia.

PPR works to support and empower Rohingya refugees through peaceful initiatives, education, community development, leadership, advocacy, and community support.

Our work is based on the belief that Rohingya community members can contribute to positive change by learning, organizing, supporting one another, and working together peacefully.

OUR VISION

A Peaceful and Empowered Rohingya Community

A peaceful, educated, and empowered Rohingya community living with dignity, unity, and hope for a just future.

OUR MISSION

Supporting Dignity, Unity & Peace

PPR empowers Rohingya refugees through education, human rights advocacy, peacebuilding, leadership development, and community support, while coordinating with humanitarian partners to promote dignity, unity, and non-violence.

OUR COMMITMENT

Working Peacefully for the Community

PPR is committed to working peacefully and responsibly to strengthen education, leadership, community participation, dignity, and support for Rohingya refugees.

We seek to cooperate with humanitarian organizations and other partners while strengthening unity and positive participation within the Rohingya community.

  • Promote education and learning opportunities.
  • Encourage youth and women leadership.
  • Support vulnerable community members.
  • Promote peaceful and non-violent engagement.
  • Strengthen community unity and awareness.
  • Cooperate with humanitarian organizations and community partners.
